# Brand Voice Analysis — About Page

## Loop 1: RESEARCH

### Brand Voice Standards

**Brand Personality:**

- The smart friend who happens to be an accountant
- Confident but not arrogant
- Direct but not blunt
- Technical but not jargon-y
- AI-Native, not SaaS

**Voice Attributes (ranked):**

1. Clear — A 12-year-old could understand
2. Confident — We make definitive statements
3. Human — Sounds like a person, not a brand
4. Smart — We understand the domain deeply
5. Warm — We care about the user's success

### Tone for About Page

**Context:** Marketing page (About)
**Tone:** Bold, aspirational, specific
**Energy:** High

## Loop 2: DEFINE

### Voice Checklist for About Page

**Voice:**

- [ ] Does it sound like a smart, confident person?
- [ ] Is it clear on first read?
- [ ] Does it avoid corporate jargon?
- [ ] Is it warm but not cheesy?

**Tone:**

- [ ] Does the tone match the context (marketing)?
- [ ] Is the energy level appropriate (high)?
- [ ] Does it sound human, not robotic?

**Terminology:**

- [ ] Are we using the right terms (AI agents, auto-categorize, human-in-the-loop)?
- [ ] Are we consistent with how we refer to features?
- [ ] Is AI terminology transparent and honest?

**Structure:**

- [ ] Is it scannable (headers, bullets, short paragraphs)?
- [ ] Does it front-load the important information?
- [ ] Are CTAs clear and action-oriented?
