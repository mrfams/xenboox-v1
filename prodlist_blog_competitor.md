## Executive Summary

Analysis of 20 competitor blogs reveals that Xenboox's Blog page must differentiate through:

1. **AI-Native Content** — Competitors don't offer AI-powered accounting insights
2. **Educational Value** — Competitors focus on product updates; we should lead with educational content
3. **Community Building** — Competitors lack community engagement features
4. **Thought Leadership** — Competitors don't position themselves as AI accounting experts

- Mix of product updates and educational content

- Categories: Accounting, Tax, Business Tips, Product Updates
