# UX Writer Analysis — About Page

## Loop 1: DISCOVER

### User Problem

Visitors to the About page need to quickly understand who Xenboox is, what we believe, and why they should trust us with their financial data. The copy must be clear, scannable, and action-oriented.

### Current Experience (Based on Previous Employees)

- PM identified 7 key sections
- Product Designer proposed 10-section layout
- UI/UX Designer defined information architecture and UX patterns
- No existing copy to audit (new page)

### Pain Points to Address

1. **Corporate jargon** — Must avoid "synergy", "leverage", "game-changing"
2. **Vague claims** — Must be specific, not "we're the best"
3. **Too much text** — Users scan, not read
4. **Inconsistent voice** — Must match Xenboox brand voice
5. **Weak CTAs** — Must drive action, not just inform

---

## Loop 2: DEFINE

### Writing Goals

1. **Clear over clever** — Understandable on first read
2. **Specific over vague** — Numbers, facts, proof
3. **Action-oriented** — Every section drives to next step
4. **Scannable** — Front-load important words
5. **On-brand** — AI-native, confident, human

### Voice by Section

| Section    | Voice              | Tone         |
| ---------- | ------------------ | ------------ |
| Hero       | Bold, confident    | Inspiring    |
| Mission    | Clear, purposeful  | Determined   |
| Story      | Human, relatable   | Empathetic   |
| Values     | Principled, direct | Confident    |
| Team       | Warm, professional | Approachable |
| Technology | Technical, precise | Expert       |
| Investors  | Subtle, credible   | Trustworthy  |
| Press      | Authoritative      | Validated    |
| CTA        | Urgent, inviting   | Encouraging  |

## Loop 5: TEST

### Copy Checklist

**Clarity:**

- ✅ Every sentence understandable on first read
- ✅ No jargon or corporate speak
- ✅ Active voice throughout
- ✅ Specific claims (numbers, facts)

**Scannability:**

- ✅ Headlines front-load important words
- ✅ Bullet points for lists
- ✅ Short paragraphs (3-4 sentences max)
- ✅ Bold key phrases

**Action-Oriented:**

- ✅ Every section drives to next step
- ✅ CTAs are clear and specific
- ✅ No dead ends

**On-Brand:**

- ✅ AI-native language ("autonomous agents", "confidence scores")
- ✅ Confident ("We believe", "We're building")
- ✅ Human ("We spent years watching", "pain firsthand")
- ✅ Specific ("10+ hours", "99.9%", "SOC 2")

**Accessibility:**

- ✅ Simple language (8th grade reading level)
- ✅ No abbreviations without context
- ✅ Descriptive link text
