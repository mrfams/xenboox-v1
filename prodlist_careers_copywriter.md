## Executive Summary

Analysis of 20 competitor Careers pages reveals that Xenboox's Careers page must use conversion-focused copy that drives applications and builds employer brand. The copy should feel like a smart, confident person explaining the future of accounting.

---

## Goal Definition

**GOAL:** Attract top talent and build employer brand through compelling careers content.

**AUDIENCE:** Talented professionals evaluating career opportunities

**CONTEXT:** Careers page — showcase culture, values, and open roles

**MEMORY:** "Xenboox is building the future of AI-native accounting."

**ACTION:** "Apply for a role" or "Join talent pool"

**SEO:** "AI-native accounting careers", "AI startup jobs", "accounting software jobs"

---

## Key Messages

1. AI-native, not just AI-powered
2. Built by accountants who understand the pain
3. Human in the loop — AI handles work, humans make decisions
4. Transparent and trustworthy — confidence scoring on every action
5. Security first — financial data is sacred

## AI-Native Copy Check

✅ Leading with AI capability ("AI-native accounting")
✅ Quantifying AI value ("500+ businesses", "10,000+ hours saved")
✅ Addressing AI fear ("confidence scoring", "human in the loop")
✅ Showing AI trust ("You approve decisions. AI executes.")
✅ Not selling SaaS ("AI-native", not "powerful automation")
✅ Using agent language ("AI-native accounting")
✅ Avoiding SaaS anti-patterns (no manual workflows AI should handle)
✅ Showing the 3-tier agent hierarchy (CFO → Department Heads → Workers)
