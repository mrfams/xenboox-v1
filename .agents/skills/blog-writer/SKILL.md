### SEO Best Practices

**Keyword Research:**

- Target long-tail keywords
- Use question-based keywords
- Consider search intent
- Analyze competitor keywords

**On-Page SEO:**

- Title tag: 50-60 characters
- Meta description: 150-160 characters
- H1: One per page, includes keyword
- H2/H3: Structure content
- Internal linking
- Image alt text

**Content Optimization:**

- Keyword density: 1-2%
- First 100 words: Include keyword
- Subheadings: Include variations
- Content length: 1500-2500 words
- Readability: Grade 8-10

### Writing Process

**1. Research**

- Target audience analysis
- Keyword research
- Competitor analysis
- Source gathering

**2. Outline**

- Hook/introduction
- Main points
- Supporting evidence
- Conclusion/CTA

**3. Draft**

- Write freely
- Don't edit while writing
- Focus on ideas
- Get words on paper

**4. Edit**

- Structural edits
- Line edits
- Proofread
- SEO optimization

**5. Optimize**

- Add visuals
- Internal links
- Meta tags
- Social sharing

### Blog Post Structure

**Template:**

```markdown
# [Keyword-Rich Title]

[Hook - 1-2 sentences that grab attention]

[Problem statement - What challenge does the reader face?]

## [H2 - Main Point 1]

[Content with examples and evidence]

## [H2 - Main Point 2]

[Content with examples and evidence]

## [H2 - Main Point 3]

[Content with examples and evidence]

## Conclusion

[Summary and CTA]

---

**About Xenboox:** [Brief pitch]
```
