**Xenboox Weaknesses:**

## Output Format

When providing competitive analysis:

1. **Competitor**: Who we're analyzing
2. **Strengths**: What they do well
3. **Weaknesses**: Where they fall short
4. **Implications**: What this means for us
5. **Recommendations**: How to compete
