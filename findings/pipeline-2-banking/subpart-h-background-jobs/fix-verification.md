## Why H1 is safe

- Excluded only `bank_statement` at the dispatch site; allowlist would have silently
  dropped `ambiguous`/`multi_type` docs that must still reach human review.
- The ingestion job gate covers every possible trigger path (document-processing,
  email-processing, retries, manual runs), so the double-booking can't return via a
  side door.
- Bank-statement money now enters the books through exactly one path:
  `import-bank-statement` → `bank_transactions` → (P2-D) `postToLedger`.
