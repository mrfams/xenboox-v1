**Actual count:** 21 agents (1 tier1 + 4 tier2 + 12 tier3 + 4 platform)
**Status:** Used 46+ times across 15+ files
**Fix:** Change to "20+ agents" or "dozens of agents" — specific counts become outdated quickly

**Status:** Used in Pricing, About, One-Pager
**Fix:** Remove or change to "Businesses across industries" — unverifiable claims hurt credibility

**Status:** Pricing page says "99%" but SLA page says "99.9%"

### 5. UNPROFESSIONAL: "19 agents, one platform" (About timeline)
**Status:** Timeline entry for Q1 2025
**Fix:** "Full agent roster complete" — more professional, avoids specific count

### 6. MISSING: No clear value proposition on some pages
**Status:** Contact page lacks a clear "why contact us" section
**Fix:** Add "What we can help with" section

### 7. WEAK CTA: "View Careers" on About page
**Status:** About page CTA is "View Careers" — not conversion-focused
**Fix:** "Join our team" or "See open positions"

### 8. INCONSISTENT: Different pages say different things about the same features
**Status:** One-pager says "19 AI agents handling invoicing, payroll, compliance, and close"
**Fix:** Standardize messaging across all pages
