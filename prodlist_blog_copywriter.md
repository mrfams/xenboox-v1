## Loop 2: RESEARCH

### Competitor Analysis

**OpenAI:** Editorial/news publication. Category tags, read time, dates. Clean, minimal.
**Anthropic:** Announcements + research. Clean editorial layout. Text-heavy.
**Digits:** Blog + podcast + case studies. Rich content.
**QuickBooks:** Business advice + product updates. SEO-focused.
**Xero:** Small business advice + product news. Clean.
**Stripe:** Developer content + industry insights. Annual letter. Press.

### Content Gaps

- No competitor emphasizes "AI-native" as explicitly as we do
- No competitor has a 3-tier agent hierarchy
- No competitor emphasizes "human in the loop" as a feature

### Key Messages

1. AI-native, not just AI-powered
2. Built by accountants who understand the pain
3. Human in the loop — AI handles work, humans make decisions
4. Transparent and trustworthy — confidence scoring on every action
5. Security first — financial data is sacred
