## Executive Summary

Large organizations—enterprises, nonprofits, construction firms, consulting companies, and government agencies—require **departmental and project-based accounting** to track financial performance at a granular level. This research covers everything Xenboox needs to support multi-department and multi-project accounting at enterprise scale.

### What Organizations Need

| Need | Why | Who |
|------|-----|-----|
| **Cost Centers** | Track expenses by department/team | All orgs |
| **Profit Centers** | Track revenue + costs by business unit | Enterprises |
| **Projects** | Track finances per engagement/contract | Consulting, Construction, Agencies |
| **Funds** | Restricted/unrestricted money tracking | Nonprofits, Governments |
| **Departments** | Hierarchical org structure in GL | All orgs with 50+ employees |
| **Allocations** | Share overhead across departments | Enterprises, Nonprofits |
| **WIP (Work-in-Progress)** | Capitalize costs until project completion | Construction, Software dev |
| **Retainage** | Hold % of payment until project complete | Construction |

## Accounting Standards & Frameworks

### GAAP (US) Requirements

**ASC 350-40 (Internal-Use Software):**
- Capitalization begins after preliminary project stage
- Three stages: Preliminary → Application Development → Post-Implementation
- Development stage costs → Capitalize (asset on balance sheet)
- Preliminary + Post-Implementation → Expense immediately
- ASU 2025-06 updates: New guidance on cloud computing costs

**ASC 985-20 (Software to be Sold/Leased):**
- Different rules for software intended for sale
- Technological feasibility → Capitalize everything after
- GAAP is more prescriptive than IFRS on timing

**ASC 606 (Revenue Recognition):**
- Performance obligations must be identified per contract
- Revenue recognized when obligations satisfied
- Project-based: recognize over time if criteria met
- Contract modifications tracked separately

**Departmental Reporting:**
- GAAP doesn't mandate departmental reporting for external filings
- BUT: Management accounting requires it for internal decision-making
- Responsibility accounting: each cost center manager accountable for their costs
- Variance analysis: budget vs actual per department

### IFRS Requirements

**IAS 38 (Intangible Assets):**
- Research costs → Always expense
- Development costs → Capitalize if 6 criteria met (PIRATE: Probable, Intention, Resources, Ability, Technical, Expenditure)
- Less prescriptive than US GAAP on timing

**IFRS 15 (Revenue from Contracts):**
- Five-step model: Identify contract → Identify obligations → Determine price → Allocate price → Recognize revenue
- Similar to ASC 606 but with different implementation details

**Construction Contracts (IAS 11 → IFRS 15):**
- Percentage of completion method for long-term contracts
- Input method (costs incurred / total estimated costs)
- Output method (milestones, deliverables)

### Industry-Specific Standards

**Construction:**
- Percentage of Completion Method (PCM) required for contracts >1 year
- Retainage: 5-10% held until final walkthrough
- CIP (Construction-in-Progress): Capitalize all costs until project completion
- Overbilling/Underbilling: Critical balance sheet items

**Nonprofit:**
- ASC 958: Financial statements must show net assets with/without donor restrictions
- Fund accounting: Separate tracking per donor restriction
- Form 990: Public disclosure of financial activities
- Restricted funds can only be used per donor intent

**Government:**
- GASB standards require fund accounting
- Modified accrual basis for governmental funds
- Budgetary accounting: encumbrances tracked alongside expenditures

**Professional Services:**
- WIP (Work-in-Progress) = unbilled revenue
- Utilization tracking: billable hours / available hours
- Project profitability: revenue vs direct costs + allocated overhead

## Departmental Accounting Model

### Department Types

| Type | Description | Tracks | Example |
|------|-------------|--------|---------|
| **Cost Center** | Incurs expenses only | Expenses | HR, IT, Legal, Admin |
| **Profit Center** | Generates revenue AND costs | Revenue + Expenses | Product lines, Stores |
| **Investment Center** | Revenue + costs + capital | All three + Assets | Divisions, Subsidiaries |
| **Revenue Center** | Generates revenue only | Revenue | Sales team |
| **Service Center** | Provides services to other depts | Internal costs | Shared Services, IT |

### Department Hierarchy

```
Organization (Root)
├── Division: North America
│   ├── Department: Sales
│   │   ├── Team: Enterprise Sales
│   │   └── Team: SMB Sales
│   ├── Department: Marketing
│   └── Department: Engineering
├── Division: Europe
│   ├── Department: Sales
│   └── Department: Engineering
└── Corporate (shared)
    ├── Department: Finance
    ├── Department: HR
    └── Department: Legal
```

**Key Requirements:**
- Departments can be nested (up to 10 levels deep for large enterprises)
- Each department has a **manager** responsible for their P&L
- Departments can be **active/inactive** (soft delete)
- Historical departments retained for audit trail
- Department codes follow organizational numbering (e.g., `ENG-001`, `SALES-WEST`)

### Department-Level Financials

Each department gets:
- **Income Statement:** Revenue, COGS, Operating Expenses, Net Income
- **Budget vs Actual:** Monthly, quarterly, annual
- **Headcount & Payroll:** Employees assigned to department
- **Asset Allocation:** Fixed assets assigned to department
- **Inter-departmental Transactions:** Services provided/received

### Responsibility Accounting

Each department manager is accountable for:
1. **Controllable Costs** — Costs they can influence (their team's expenses)
2. **Non-Controllable Costs** — Allocated overhead (shown but not blamed)
3. **Performance Metrics:**
   - Budget Variance (% over/under budget)
   - Cost per Employee
   - Revenue per Employee (profit centers only)
   - Utilization Rate (service centers)

## Project Accounting Model

### Project Types

| Type | Description | Revenue Model | Industry |
|------|-------------|---------------|----------|
| **Fixed Price** | Agreed price upfront | Recognized on completion or milestones | Consulting, Construction |
| **Time & Materials** | Bill by hours + expenses | Monthly invoicing | Consulting, Legal |
| **Cost Plus** | Costs + agreed margin | Reimbursement + fee | Government, Construction |
| **Subscription** | Recurring payments | Monthly/annual revenue | SaaS, Services |
| **Grant-Funded** | Donor/government funded | Reimbursements per milestones | Nonprofit, Research |
| **Internal** | No external revenue | Cost tracking only | All orgs |

### Project Lifecycle

```
1. PLANNING
   ├── Project proposal / SOW
   ├── Budget estimation
   ├── Resource planning
   └── Contract/sign-off

2. EXECUTION
   ├── Time tracking
   ├── Expense tracking
   ├── Progress billing (if applicable)
   ├── WIP capitalization (if applicable)
   └── Milestone tracking

3. MONITORING
   ├── Budget vs actual (variance analysis)
   ├── EAC (Estimate at Completion)
   ├── ETC (Estimate to Complete)
   ├── CPI (Cost Performance Index)
   └── SPI (Schedule Performance Index)

4. CLOSURE
   ├── Final invoicing
   ├── Retainage release (if applicable)
   ├── WIP → Asset transfer (if applicable)
   ├── Lessons learned
   └── Project archival
```

### Project Financial Tracking

**Per-Project Income Statement:**
```
Revenue
  ├── Contract Value
  ├── Change Orders
  └── Total Revenue
Cost of Goods Sold
  ├── Direct Labor
  ├── Direct Materials
  ├── Subcontractors
  └── Travel & Expenses
Gross Profit
Overhead Allocation (if applicable)
Project Net Income
Margin % = Net Income / Revenue
```

### Work-in-Progress (WIP)

WIP represents costs incurred but not yet billable or recognized as revenue.

**When WIP Applies:**
- Construction projects (always)
- Software development (capitalize per ASC 350-40)
- Long-term consulting contracts
- Manufacturing orders

**WIP Accounting Entries:**
```
During project:
  Dr. WIP Asset (Balance Sheet)
  Cr. Cash/Payable (when costs incurred)

When project completes:
  Dr. Fixed Asset / Inventory
  Cr. WIP Asset

Revenue Recognition:
  Dr. Accounts Receivable / Cash
  Cr. Revenue
```

**Overbilling vs Underbilling:**
- **Overbilled:** Billed more than costs recognized → Liability (deferred revenue)
- **Underbilled:** Costs recognized more than billed → Asset (WIP)
- Critical for construction and long-term contracts

### Retainage

- Client holds 5-10% of each progress payment
- Released upon project completion and final walkthrough
- Tracked as a receivable until released
- Must be separately reported in financial statements

### Project-Level KPIs

| KPI | Formula | Target |
|-----|---------|--------|
| **Gross Margin** | (Revenue - Direct Costs) / Revenue | >30% |
| **Utilization Rate** | Billable Hours / Available Hours | >75% |
| **Cost Performance Index (CPI)** | Earned Value / Actual Cost | ≥1.0 |
| **Schedule Performance Index (SPI)** | Earned Value / Planned Value | ≥1.0 |
| **Estimate at Completion (EAC)** | BAC / CPI | Within budget |
| **Burn Rate** | Actual Spend / Time Period | On plan |
| **Project Duration** | Actual End - Actual Start | On schedule |

## Cost Allocation Methods

### Allocation Bases

| Method | Formula | Best For |
|--------|---------|----------|
| **Direct Allocation** | Costs assigned directly to departments | Costs traceable to one dept |
| **Step-Down** | Sequential allocation from service → operating | Shared services |
| **Reciprocal** | Mutual services between departments | Complex organizations |
| **Activity-Based (ABC)** | Costs assigned to activities → activities to departments | High accuracy |
| **Square Footage** | Costs / total sq ft × dept sq ft | Rent, utilities |
| **Headcount** | Costs / total employees × dept employees | HR, benefits |
| **Revenue** | Costs / total revenue × dept revenue | Marketing, admin |
| **Machine Hours** | Costs / total hours × dept hours | Manufacturing |
| **Time-Based** | Costs / total time × dept time | Shared IT, legal |

### Overhead Allocation Rules

1. **Service departments** allocate to **operating departments** only
2. **Operating departments** do NOT allocate back to service departments (unless reciprocal)
3. Allocation percentages reviewed quarterly
4. **Materiality threshold:** Don't allocate amounts < $100
5. **Audit trail:** Every allocation logged with basis, percentage, amount

### Inter-Departmental Pricing

When departments provide services to each other:
- **Market Rate:** Price at what external vendor would charge
- **Cost Plus:** Cost + agreed margin (typically 5-15%)
- **Negotiated Rate:** Agreed between department managers
- **Transfer Price Must:** Comply with tax regulations (arm's length principle)

## Intercompany & Transfer Pricing

### When This Applies

- Multi-entity organizations (parent + subsidiaries)
- Departments that span legal entities
- Cross-border operations
- Shared service centers serving multiple entities

### Transfer Pricing Methods (OECD/GAAP)

1. **Comparable Uncontrolled Price (CUP):** Market price for similar transactions
2. **Resale Price Method:** Reseller's margin deducted from resale price
3. **Cost Plus Method:** Supplier's costs + markup
4. **Transactional Net Margin Method (TNMM):** Net margin comparison
5. **Profit Split Method:** Split profits based on contribution

### Elimination Entries

When consolidating financials:
```
Dr. Intercompany Revenue (Parent)
Cr. Intercompany Expense (Subsidiary)

Dr. Intercompany Payable (Subsidiary)
Cr. Intercompany Receivable (Parent)
```

**Key Rule:** Intercompany transactions MUST balance to zero in consolidation.

## Nonprofit & Fund Accounting

### Fund Types

| Fund Type | Description | Can Be Spent Freely? |
|-----------|-------------|----------------------|
| **Unrestricted** | General operating funds | ✅ Yes |
| **Temporarily Restricted** | Donor restrictions on time or purpose | ⚠️ When conditions met |
| **Permanently Restricted** | Endowments (principal preserved) | ❌ Only investment income |

### Grant Accounting

Each grant is essentially a "project" with:
- **Budget:** Total grant amount
- **Period:** Start/end dates
- **Restrictions:** What funds can be spent on
- **Reporting:** Required reports to donor
- **Compliance:** Must meet grant terms
- **Drawdown:** Reimbursement schedule

### Fund Accounting Entries

```
Receipt of Restricted Grant:
  Dr. Cash
  Cr. Contribution Revenue — With Donor Restrictions

Satisfying Restriction (e.g., spending on allowed purpose):
  Dr. Net Assets With Donor Restrictions — Reclassifications
  Cr. Net Assets Without Donor Restrictions — Reclassifications

Expense on Restricted Grant:
  Dr. Program Expense
  Cr. Cash/Accounts Payable
```

### Compliance Requirements

- **Form 990:** Annual filing with IRS (nonprofits)
- **Single Audit:** Required if federal funds >$750,000
- **Donor Reporting:** Per grant terms (quarterly, annual)
- **Board Reporting:** Monthly financials by fund
- **Segregation of Duties:** Different people approve vs record

## Security & Access Control

### Permission Model

```
permissions:
  departments:
    - departments:create      — Create new departments
    - departments:read        — View department data
    - departments:update      — Edit department details
    - departments:delete      — Soft-delete departments
    - departments:manage_budget — Set department budgets
    - departments:allocate    — Run cost allocations
    - departments:report      — View departmental reports

  projects:
    - projects:create         — Create new projects
    - projects:read           — View project data
    - projects:update         — Edit project details
    - projects:delete         — Soft-delete projects
    - projects:manage_budget  — Set project budgets
    - projects:bill           — Create project invoices
    - projects:close          — Close projects
    - projects:report         — View project reports

  funds:
    - funds:create            — Create funds (restricted access)
    - funds:read              — View fund data
    - funds:reclassify        — Move between fund types
    - funds:report            — View fund reports

  wip:
    - wip:create              — Record WIP entries
    - wip:read                — View WIP data
    - wip:post                — Post WIP to GL
```

### Data Isolation Rules

1. **Entity Isolation:** All department/project data scoped to entity_id (existing rule)
2. **Department Isolation:** Users see only departments they have access to
   - Admin: All departments
   - Manager: Own department + sub-departments
   - Staff: Own department only
3. **Project Isolation:** Users see only projects they're assigned to
   - Admin: All projects
   - Manager: Assigned projects
   - Staff: Assigned projects (read-only)
4. **Fund Restriction:** Fund data visible only to Finance team + fund manager
5. **Intercompany:** Elimination entries visible only to consolidation users

| Role | Departments | Projects | Funds | WIP | Allocations |
|------|------------|----------|-------|-----|-------------|
| **Admin** | CRUD | CRUD | CRUD | CRUD | Approve |

| **Staff** | Read (own) | Read (assigned) | — | — | — |
| **Auditor** | Read (all) | Read (all) | Read (all) | Read (all) | Read (all) |

### Audit Trail Requirements

Every department/project action must log:
- **Who** performed the action (user ID)
- **What** was changed (field, old value, new value)
- **When** it happened (timestamp)
- **Why** (reason for change, if required)
- **Entity** scope (entity_id)
- **Department/Project** scope (department_id, project_id)

## Compliance & Regulatory

### GAAP Compliance Checklist

- [ ] Departmental P&L follows responsibility accounting principles
- [ ] WIP capitalized per ASC 350-40 (internal-use) or ASC 985-20 (for sale)
- [ ] Revenue recognition per ASC 606 with performance obligations per project
- [ ] Intercompany transactions eliminated in consolidation
- [ ] Cost allocations documented with clear methodology
- [ ] Budget variances reported with explanations
- [ ] Fund accounting follows ASC 958 (nonprofit)

### IFRS Compliance Checklist

- [ ] Project revenue per IFRS 15 five-step model
- [ ] Development costs capitalized per IAS 38 criteria
- [ ] Transfer pricing arm's length principle followed
- [ ] Departmental reporting per management accounting needs

### Audit Requirements

- [ ] Every journal entry tagged with department_id, project_id, fund_id
- [ ] Cost allocation methodology documented and versioned
- [ ] Budget changes require approval workflow
- [ ] WIP balance reconciliation monthly
- [ ] Intercompany balances reconcile to zero
- [ ] Fund balances reconcile to restrictions
- [ ] Retainage tracked separately from receivables

### Tax Implications

- **Transfer Pricing:** Must comply with arm's length principle (OECD guidelines)
- **Cost Allocations:** Must be reasonable and consistently applied
- **WIP Capitalization:** Affects taxable income timing
- **Grant Revenue:** May be taxable depending on type and jurisdiction
- **Intercompany Eliminations:** Required for consolidated tax filings

### What We're Actually Building

It's **8 new database tables** and **3 modified tables**, not a new product:

```
8 NEW TABLES:
  departments, projects, project_milestones,
  cost_allocations, wip_entries, project_budgets,
  department_budgets, funds, fund_transactions

3 MODIFIED TABLES (add columns):
  journal_entries  → +department_id, +project_id, +fund_id
  invoices         → +department_id, +project_id, +milestone_id, +retainage
  expenses         → +department_id, +project_id, +fund_id
```

### Why It's Feasible

1. **It's mostly plumbing** — adding foreign keys, new tables, and new UI pages. The core accounting engine already works.
2. **We follow existing patterns** — every table gets entity_id scoping, every procedure gets validation, every component follows Shadcn/ui.
3. **Agents are extensible** — adding a new tool to CFO Agent takes hours, not weeks.
4. **UI reuses existing components** — Card, Table, Dialog, Badge, Chart components already exist.
5. **Cost allocation is pure math** — 5 allocation methods are algorithms, not complex integrations.

### What Could Slow Us Down

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Recursive department hierarchy queries (CTEs) | Neon PostgreSQL supports recursive CTEs natively — index on parent_id |
| WIP balance calculations at scale | Background job via Trigger.dev, not real-time |
| Nonprofit fund compliance (Form 990) | Defer to Phase 2 if needed — core fund tracking works without it |
| Intercompany elimination entries | Complex but isolated — only affects consolidation reports |
| Multi-currency projects | Our existing currency system handles this — just wire it up |
