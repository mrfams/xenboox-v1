## Executive Summary

Analysis of 20 competitor Careers pages reveals that Xenboox's Careers page must differentiate through:

1. **AI-Native Culture** — Competitors don't offer AI-native accounting careers
2. **Career Growth** — Competitors focus on traditional career paths; we should lead with AI-native growth
3. **Team Culture** — Competitors lack AI-native team culture features
4. **Thought Leadership** — Competitors don't position themselves as AI accounting experts
