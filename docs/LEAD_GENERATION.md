# Lead Generation Channels

> How Xenboox generates qualified leads in The Gambia.

---

## Channel 1: Content Marketing (SEO)

### Tactics

- Blog posts targeting "accounting software Gambia", "AI accounting Africa"
- Comparison pages: "Xenboox vs QuickBooks", "Xenboox vs Xero"
- FAQ pages for common accounting questions
- Technical docs for developer audience

### Lead Capture

- Blog CTA: "Start Free Trial"
- Newsletter signup on blog pages
- Gated content: "Gambian Tax Compliance Guide" (PDF download)

### Expected Leads: 50/month (organic)

---

## Channel 2: LinkedIn

### Tactics

- Founder personal brand posts (2-3x/week)
- Company page updates (1x/week)
- Targeted outreach to CFOs and finance leaders
- Accounting tips and AI insights

### Lead Capture

- LinkedIn profile CTA: "Book a demo"
- Post CTAs: "Start Free Trial"
- Direct messages to qualified prospects

### Expected Leads: 20/month

---

## Channel 3: Partnerships

### Tactics

- Accounting firm referral program (20% revenue share)
- Bank integration partnerships
- NGO network partnerships
- Technology partner co-marketing

### Lead Capture

- Partner referral links
- Co-branded landing pages
- Joint webinars

### Expected Leads: 10/month

---

## Channel 4: Events & Webinars

### Tactics

- Monthly webinar: "AI Accounting 101"
- Quarterly in-person meetup in Banjul
- Sponsor local business events
- Speak at accounting conferences

### Lead Capture

- Event registration forms
- Post-event follow-up emails
- Demo booking at events

### Expected Leads: 15/month

---

## Channel 5: Paid Advertising

### Tactics (Phase 2 — after organic traction)

- Google Ads for high-intent keywords
- LinkedIn Ads targeting finance professionals
- Facebook Ads for broader awareness

### Lead Capture

- Landing page with clear CTA
- Retargeting for website visitors
- Lead magnets (guides, templates)

### Expected Leads: 30/month (when active)

## Lead Scoring

| Score        | Criteria                             | Action                 |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------ | ---------------------- |

| Warm (50-79) | Downloaded content, visited 3+ pages | Email nurture sequence |
| Cold (< 50)  | Single page view                     | Monthly newsletter     |
