# Content Distribution Strategy

> How to get Xenboox content in front of the right audience.

---

## Channels

### 1. SEO (Organic Search)

- **Target:** 10K monthly organic visitors
- **Tactics:**
  - Publish 2-4 blog posts/month targeting long-tail keywords
  - Comparison pages for QuickBooks, Xero, Wave
  - FAQ pages for common accounting questions
  - Technical docs for developer audience
- **Current assets:** 6 blog posts, comparison pages, FAQ, docs

### 2. LinkedIn

- **Target:** 1K followers, 50 engagement/week
- **Tactics:**
  - Founder personal brand posts (2-3x/week)
  - Company page updates (1x/week)
  - employee spotlights and customer stories
  - Accounting tips and AI insights
- **Current state:** Not started

### 3. Email Marketing

- **Target:** 2K subscribers, 30% open rate
- **Tactics:**
  - Weekly newsletter with product updates and accounting tips
  - Drip sequences for new signups
  - Re-engagement campaigns for inactive users
- **Current assets:** Newsletter form, retention email templates

### 4. Social Media (Twitter/X, Facebook)

- **Target:** 5K followers combined
- **Tactics:**
  - Product updates and feature announcements
  - Accounting tips and industry news
  - Customer success stories
  - Behind-the-scenes of building in The Gambia
- **Current state:** Not started

### 5. Partnerships & Referrals

- **Target:** 10 partner referrals/month
- **Tactics:**
  - Accounting firm partner program
  - Bank integration partnerships
  - Referral incentives for existing customers
- **Current assets:** /for-accountants page

### 6. Events & Webinars

- **Target:** 2 events/quarter
- **Tactics:**
  - Monthly webinar: "AI Accounting 101"
  - Quarterly in-person meetup in Banjul
  - Sponsor local business events
- **Current state:** Not started
