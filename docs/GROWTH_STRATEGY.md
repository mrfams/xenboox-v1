# Growth Strategy

> How Xenboox acquires, activates, and retains customers.

---

## Growth Channels

### 1. Content Marketing (SEO)

- **Target keywords:** "accounting software Gambia", "AI accounting Africa", "QuickBooks alternative Gambia"
- **Content types:** Blog posts, comparison pages, tutorials, case studies
- **Goal:** 10K organic monthly visitors within 12 months
- **Current state:** 6 blog posts, comparison pages, FAQ, docs

### 2. Product-Led Growth (PLG)

- **Free tier** as acquisition funnel
- **In-product virality:** Client portal, team invites, shareable reports
- **Goal:** 20% free-to-paid conversion within 6 months
- **Current state:** Free tier with 1 entity, 50 journal entries/month

### 3. Partnerships

- **Accounting firms:** Referral program, white-label option
- **Banks:** Integration partnerships (Plaid, Mono)
- **Goal:** 10 partner referrals/month within 12 months
- **Current state:** /for-accountants page, comparison pages

### 4. Community

- **Slack/Discord** for users and partners
- **Webinars** for education and demos
- **Goal:** 500 community members within 12 months
- **Current state:** Not started

### 5. Outbound Sales

- **LinkedIn outreach** to CFOs and finance leaders
- **Events** in Banjul and Serrekunda
- **Goal:** 5 qualified leads/week
- **Current state:** Not started

## Retention Strategy

1. **Daily value:** AI insights delivered proactively
2. **Weekly value:** Automated reports and reconciliation
3. **Monthly value:** Month-end close automation
4. **Quarterly value:** QBR template and business review
