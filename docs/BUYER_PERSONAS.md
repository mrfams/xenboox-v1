# Buyer Personas

> Target personas for Xenboox marketing, sales, and product decisions.

---

## Persona 1: The Overwhelmed CFO

### Demographics

- **Title:** CFO, Finance Director, Head of Finance
- **Company size:** 20-200 employees
- **Industry:** Trading, logistics, professional services
- **Location:** Banjul, Serrekunda, Brikama

### Goals

- Close books faster (currently 2-3 weeks)
- Reduce accounting headcount costs
- Get accurate financial reports on time
- Comply with NAPSA, VAT, and tax filings

### Pain Points

- Month-end close takes too long
- Accountants make manual data entry errors
- Multi-currency reconciliation is a nightmare
- Can't trust the numbers in reports

### Objections

- "We already have QuickBooks"
- "AI can't handle our complex transactions"
- "What about data security?"

### How to Reach

- LinkedIn outreach to finance leaders
- CFO roundtable events in Banjul
- Content: "How to close your books in 4 days"

## Persona 2: The Growth-Stage Founder

### Demographics

- **Title:** CEO, Founder, Managing Director
- **Company size:** 5-50 employees
- **Industry:** FMCG, food & beverage, agriculture
- **Location:** The Gambia, West Africa

### Goals

- Focus on growing the business, not accounting
- Hire fewer back-office staff
- Understand cash flow without an accountant
- Look professional to investors and donors

### Pain Points

- Can't afford a full-time accountant
- Spreadsheets are error-prone
- Doesn't understand financial statements
- Missed tax deadlines

### Objections

- "Is it affordable for a startup?"
- "I don't know accounting — will the AI confuse me?"
- "Does it work in The Gambia?"

### How to Reach

- Startup ecosystem events
- Content: "AI accounting for founders who hate accounting"
- Free tier as entry point

## Persona 3: The Accounting Firm Partner

### Demographics

- **Title:** Partner, Senior Accountant, Practice Manager
- **Firm size:** 3-15 accountants
- **Clients:** 20-100 SMBs
- **Location:** The Gambia, West Africa

### Goals

- Manage more clients without hiring
- Automate repetitive bookkeeping tasks
- Offer advisory services (not just compliance)
- Standardize processes across clients

### Pain Points

- Each client uses different software
- Bookkeeping takes 70% of team time
- Can't scale without hiring more staff
- Clients expect instant responses

### Objections

- "My clients won't switch from QuickBooks"
- "What if the AI makes mistakes on client books?"
- "I need to maintain professional standards"

### How to Reach

- Gambia Institute of Chartered Accountants
- Content: "How AI helps firms manage 40+ entities"
- Partner program with referral incentives

## Persona 4: The Donor/NGO Manager

### Demographics

- **Title:** Program Manager, Finance Officer, Grants Manager
- **Organization:** NGO, donor-funded project
- **Budget:** GMD 500K - 50M
- **Location:** The Gambia, West Africa

### Goals

- Track grant spending against budgets
- Generate donor reports on time
- Ensure compliance with donor requirements
- Maintain transparent financial records

### Pain Points

- Donor reports take weeks to prepare
- Budget vs actual tracking is manual
- Multiple funding sources with different rules
- Audit readiness is stressful

### Objections

- "We need donor-specific reporting formats"
- "Our donors require specific accounting standards"
- "Can it handle project-based accounting?"

### How to Reach

- Donor coordination meetings
- Content: "Automate donor reporting with AI"
- Integration with donor management systems
