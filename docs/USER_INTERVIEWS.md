# User Interviews Process

> Structured process for conducting user interviews to validate product decisions.

---

## Interview Cadence

| Type      | Frequency | Duration | Participants           |
| --------- | --------- | -------- | ---------------------- |
| Discovery | Monthly   | 30 min   | 3-5 new users          |
| Usability | Bi-weekly | 45 min   | 2-3 existing users     |
| Churn     | As needed | 20 min   | Recently churned users |
| Win       | As needed | 30 min   | New paid customers     |
| Loss      | As needed | 20 min   | Lost prospects         |

---

## Interview Scripts

### Discovery Interview (New Users)

1. Tell me about your business and how you manage accounting today.
2. What tools do you currently use? What do you like/dislike about them?
3. How much time does your team spend on bookkeeping each week?
4. What's the most frustrating part of your current process?
5. If you could wave a magic wand, what would accounting look like?
6. Would you be interested in trying an AI-powered alternative?

### Usability Interview (Existing Users)

1. Walk me through how you use Xenboox on a typical day.
2. What feature do you use most? Least?
3. Have you encountered any confusing or frustrating moments?
4. How does Xenboox compare to what you used before?
5. What would make you recommend Xenboox to a colleague?
6. What's one thing you wish Xenboox could do?

### Churn Interview (Lost Users)

1. What made you decide to stop using Xenboox?
2. Was there a specific moment or issue that triggered your decision?
3. What did you switch to? Why?
4. Is there anything we could have done differently?
5. Would you consider coming back if we addressed [specific issue]?

## Interview Logistics

### Recruitment

- **Source:** PostHog user segments, CRM, support tickets
- **Incentive:** GMD 200 gift card or 1 month free
- **Scheduling:** Calendly link in email outreach

### Recording & Analysis

- Record with consent (Zoom/Google Meet)
- Take notes in shared doc
- Tag themes in Notion/Airtable
- Share insights in weekly product sync
