# Product-Market Fit Measurement

> How Xenboox measures and tracks product-market fit.

---

## Definition

Product-market fit exists when:

1. Users actively use the product without being prompted
2. Users would be "very disappointed" if the product disappeared
3. Organic growth exceeds paid acquisition
4. Retention curves flatten above 40% at Day 30

---

## Measurement Framework

### 1. Sean Ellis Survey

**Question:** "How would you feel if you could no longer use Xenboox?"

| Response                | Target | Current |
| ----------------------- | ------ | ------- |
| Very disappointed       | > 40%  | —       |
| Somewhat disappointed   | 20-30% | —       |
| Not disappointed        | < 20%  | —       |
| N/A — don't use anymore | < 10%  | —       |

**Run quarterly** to all active users.

### 2. Activation Rate

**Definition:** % of signups who complete 3+ key actions within 7 days

Key actions:

- Connect bank account
- Create first invoice
- Run first report
- Process first payroll
- Approve first AI recommendation

**Target:** 30% activation rate

### 3. Retention Curves

**Definition:** % of users who return after N days

| Cohort  | Day 1 | Day 7 | Day 30 | Day 90 |
| ------- | ----- | ----- | ------ | ------ |
| Target  | 80%   | 50%   | 40%    | 30%    |
| Current | —     | —     | —      | —      |

### 4. Organic Growth Ratio

**Definition:** Organic signups / Total signups

**Target:** > 60% organic

### 5. NPS (Net Promoter Score)

**Definition:** % Promoters (9-10) - % Detractors (0-6)

**Target:** > 50

**Current:** Quarterly NPS survey implemented ✅

---

## Tracking Cadence

| Metric            | Frequency | Owner            |
| ----------------- | --------- | ---------------- |
| Sean Ellis Survey | Quarterly | Product          |
| Activation Rate   | Weekly    | Analytics        |
| Retention Curves  | Monthly   | Analytics        |
| Organic Growth    | Monthly   | Marketing        |
| NPS               | Quarterly | Customer Success |

---

## What PMF Looks Like in Data

| Signal                         | PMF        | No PMF     |
| ------------------------------ | ---------- | ---------- |
| Retention D30                  | > 40%      | < 20%      |
| NPS                            | > 50       | < 20       |
| Sean Ellis "very disappointed" | > 40%      | < 25%      |
| Organic/Paid ratio             | > 2:1      | < 1:1      |
| Support tickets per user       | Decreasing | Increasing |
