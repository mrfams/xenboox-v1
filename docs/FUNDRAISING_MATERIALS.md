## Pitch Deck Structure

### Slide 1: Title

- Xenboox: AI-Native Accounting for Africa
- Tagline: "19 AI agents do the work. Humans make decisions."

- Accounting in Africa is manual, slow, and error-prone
- SMBs spend 20+ hours/month on bookkeeping
- QuickBooks/Xero not built for African markets (mobile money, multi-currency)

### Slide 3: Solution

- AI-native accounting platform
- 19 specialized agents handle invoicing, payroll, compliance, close
- Built for The Gambia and West Africa
