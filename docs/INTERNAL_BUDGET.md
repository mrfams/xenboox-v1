## Cost Optimization Priorities

1. **Use free tiers** — Most services have generous free tiers
2. **Pay only when necessary** — Upgrade only when free tier limits are hit
3. **Monitor monthly** — Review spend in Vercel, Neon, Trigger.dev dashboards
4. **Set alerts** — Vercel cost alerts at $50, $100, $200
