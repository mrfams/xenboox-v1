# Gambian Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

> Who Xenboox is built for in The Gambia market.

---

## Primary ICP: Growing SMBs

### Profile

- **Revenue:** GMD 5M - 200M/year ($70K - $2.8M)
- **Employees:** 10-100
- **Sectors:** Trading, logistics, FMCG, professional services, agriculture
- **Location:** Banjul, Serrekunda, Brikama, Brusubi
- **Current tools:** Spreadsheets, QuickBooks, manual ledgers

### Decision Maker

- **Title:** Owner, Managing Director, CFO
- **Age:** 30-55
- **Pain:** Drowning in bookkeeping, can't trust the numbers, missing deadlines
- **Budget:** GMD 1,500 - 10,000/month ($20 - $140)
- **Buying trigger:** Missed tax filing, failed audit, investor pressure

### Why Xenboox Wins

1. Multi-currency built-in (GMD, USD, EUR, GBP)
2. Mobile money integration (M-Pesa, Airtel Money)
3. AI handles the work — no accounting expertise needed
4. Affordability — free tier to start, flat pricing

---

## Secondary ICP: Accounting Firms

### Profile

- **Size:** 3-15 accountants
- **Clients:** 20-100 SMBs
- **Revenue:** GMD 2M - 20M/year
- **Current tools:** QuickBooks, spreadsheets, paper files

### Decision Maker

- **Title:** Partner, Senior Accountant
- **Pain:** Can't scale without hiring, each client uses different software
- **Budget:** GMD 3,000 - 15,000/month ($40 - $210)

### Why Xenboox Wins

1. Multi-entity management from one dashboard
2. Unlimited clients, no per-seat fees
3. Standardized processes across all clients
4. Client portal for external stakeholders

---

## Tertiary ICP: NGOs & Donor-Funded Projects

### Profile

- **Budget:** GMD 500K - 50M in grant funding
- **Staff:** 5-50
- **Sectors:** Education, health, agriculture, governance
- **Current tools:** Spreadsheets, donor-specific templates

### Decision Maker

- **Title:** Program Manager, Finance Officer
- **Pain:** Donor reports take weeks, budget tracking is manual
- **Budget:** GMD 2,000 - 8,000/month ($30 - $110)

### Why Xenboox Wins

1. Automated donor reporting
2. Budget vs actual tracking
3. Project-based accounting
4. Audit-ready financial records
