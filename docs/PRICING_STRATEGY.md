# Pricing Strategy

> How Xenboox prices its plans for maximum adoption and revenue.

---

## Current Pricing

| Plan     | Price  | Entities | Users     | AI Agents |
| -------- | ------ | -------- | --------- | --------- |
| Free     | $0/mo  | 1        | 1         | 1 (CFO)   |
| Starter  | $29/mo | 3        | Unlimited | All 19    |
| Business | $79/mo | 10       | Unlimited | All 19    |

---

## Why This Pricing Works

### Free Tier

- Low barrier to entry
- AI value demonstration
- Upgrade trigger at 50 journal entries

### Starter ($29/mo)

- Below QuickBooks ($30/mo)
- Unlimited users (QuickBooks charges per seat)
- Full AI workforce

### Business ($79/mo)

- Multi-entity consolidation
- Multi-currency support
- Priority support
