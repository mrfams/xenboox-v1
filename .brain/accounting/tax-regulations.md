# Tax Regulations by Market

## The Gambia (Launch Market)

### VAT (Sales Tax)

- Standard rate: 15%
- Zero-rated: Basic food items, medical supplies, educational materials
- Exempt: Financial services, rental property
- Threshold: GMD 1,000,000 annual turnover
- Filing: Monthly, due by 15th of following month

### Corporate Income Tax

- Rate: 27%
- Filing: Annual, 6 months after year-end
- Withholding tax: 10% on dividends, 15% on royalties

### PAYE (Payroll Tax)

- Employer: 5% of gross salary
- Employee: Progressive (0-15%)
- Filing: Monthly

## Nigeria (Planned Expansion)

### VAT

- Standard rate: 7.5%
- Filing: Monthly

### Corporate Income Tax

- Rate: 30%
- Filing: Annual

## Ghana (Planned Expansion)

### VAT

- Standard rate: 12.5%
- Filing: Quarterly
