# GAAP Accounting Policies — Xenboox

## Double-Entry Principle

Every transaction records equal debits and credits. Ledger Agent enforces this at posting time.

## Revenue Recognition

Revenue recognized when earned, not when cash received (accrual basis).

## Expense Recognition

Expenses matched to the period they help generate revenue.

## Asset Valuation

Fixed assets recorded at cost, depreciated over useful life using straight-line method.

## Currency

Functional currency: GMD (Gambian Dalasi). Multi-currency transactions converted at spot rate. Unrealized gains/losses recorded at period end.

## Entity Scoping

All financial records scoped to an entity_id. No cross-entity contamination.
