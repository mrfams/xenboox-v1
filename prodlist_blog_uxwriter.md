# UX Writer Analysis — Blog Page

## Loop 1: DISCOVER

### User Problem

SMEs and accounting professionals need to find educational content, product updates, and industry insights. The Blog page microcopy must be clear, scannable, and action-oriented.

### Current Experience (Based on Product Designer & UI/UX Designer Analysis)

- Product Designer proposed 10-section layout
- UI/UX Designer defined navigation and UX patterns
- Blog structure: /blog + /blog/[slug]
- Categories: Product, Engineering, AI, Accounting, Company

### Pain Points to Address

1. **Content discovery** — Users need to find relevant content quickly
2. **Content scanning** — Users need to scan titles and excerpts
3. **Content reading** — Users need a comfortable reading experience
4. **Content navigation** — Users need to move between posts easily
5. **Newsletter signup** — Users need to subscribe easily

---

## Loop 2: DEFINE

### Writing Goals

1. **Clear over clever** — Understandable on first read
2. **Specific over vague** — Numbers, facts, proof
3. **Action-oriented** — Every section drives to next step
4. **Scannable** — Front-load important words
5. **On-brand** — AI-native, confident, human

### Voice by Section

| Section          | Voice                  | Tone        |
| ---------------- | ---------------------- | ----------- |
| Hero             | Bold, confident        | Inspiring   |
| Featured Post    | Engaging, intriguing   | Curious     |
| Category Filters | Clear, functional      | Helpful     |

## Loop 5: TEST

### Copy Checklist

**Clarity:**

- ✅ Every sentence understandable on first read
- ✅ No jargon or corporate speak
- ✅ Active voice throughout
- ✅ Specific claims (numbers, facts)

**Scannability:**

- ✅ Headlines front-load important words
- ✅ Bullet points for lists
- ✅ Short paragraphs (3-4 sentences max)
- ✅ Bold key phrases

**Action-Oriented:**

- ✅ Every section drives to next step
- ✅ CTAs are clear and specific
- ✅ No dead ends

**On-Brand:**

- ✅ AI-native language ("AI-native accounting")
- ✅ Confident ("Insights for AI-native accounting")
- ✅ Human ("Expert insights on AI, accounting, and the future of finance")
- ✅ Specific ("X min read", "500+ businesses")

**Accessibility:**

- ✅ Simple language (8th grade reading level)
- ✅ No abbreviations without context
- ✅ Descriptive link text
