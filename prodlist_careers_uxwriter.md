## AI-Native Copy Patterns

### Confidence Indicators

- "AI matches your skills to this role with 94% confidence"
- "AI suggests this role based on your experience (87% match)"
- "AI is very confident you'd be a great fit for this role (96%)"

### Agent Activity

- "AI is analyzing your application..."
- "AI is matching your skills to open roles..."
- "AI is preparing your personalized career path..."

### Decision Cards

- "AI recommends applying for this role. Apply?"
- "AI found 3 roles that match your skills. View them?"
- "AI suggests updating your profile to improve matches. Update?"

### Narrative Flow

- "AI analyzed 127 roles and found 5 that match your skills. Here's what it found:"
- "AI detected a trend in your career path. Let me explain:"
- "AI is ready to help you find the perfect role. Starting now..."

### Proactive Alerts

- "AI noticed your skills match a new role. Want to apply?"
- "AI found 2 roles that just opened in your preferred location. View them?"
- "AI detected a potential career growth opportunity. Review?"

### Loading States

- "AI is thinking..."
- "AI is analyzing your profile..."
- "AI is finding the best roles for you..."

### Error States

- "AI couldn't match your skills to any roles. Try broadening your search."
- "AI encountered an error. Here's what happened and what to do:"
- "AI needs more information. Can you provide your preferred location?"

### Empty States

- "AI hasn't found any matching roles yet. Try updating your profile."
- "AI is ready to help you find the perfect role. What are you looking for?"
- "AI is learning your preferences. The more you use it, the better it gets."
