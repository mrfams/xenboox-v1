## Loop 4: SYNTHESIZE

### Competitive Insights for About Page

**Insight 1: Mission-Driven Wins**

- OpenAI and Anthropic lead with mission
- Mission builds emotional connection
- **Recommendation:** Lead with mission ("AI-native accounting that works for you")

**Insight 2: Safety/Trust is Critical**

- Anthropic emphasizes safety and governance
- Trust is essential for financial data
- **Recommendation:** Emphasize security and transparency

**Insight 3: Scale Signals Credibility**

- Stripe and QuickBooks use scale metrics
- Numbers build trust
- **Recommendation:** Include stats bar (500+ businesses, $2B+ managed)

**Insight 4: Customer-Centricity Resonates**

- Xero leads with customer count
- Customer success stories build trust
- **Recommendation:** Include customer count and testimonials

**Insight 5: AI-Native is Differentiating**

- Digits positions as AI-native
- Xenboox is truly AI-native (not bolt-on AI)
- **Recommendation:** Emphasize "AI-native, not AI-powered"
