# Marketing Psychology Analysis — About Page

## Loop 1: RESEARCH

### Customer Psychology

**Trust Deficit:**

- Visitors are skeptical of new companies
- Financial data requires extreme trust
- AI adds another layer of concern ("Is AI safe for accounting?")

**Decision Factors:**

- Security: "Can I trust them with my financial data?"
- Competence: "Do they know what they're doing?"
- Reliability: "Will they be around in 5 years?"
- Value: "Is this better than what I have?"

---

## Loop 2: DEFINE

### Psychological Principles to Apply

**Primary Principles:**

1. Social Proof — Show others trust us
2. Authority — Show expertise and competence
3. Trust Signals — Build credibility
4. Loss Aversion — Show what they're missing
5. Reciprocity — Give value before asking

**Secondary Principles:**

1. Anchoring — Set expectations with stats
2. Framing — Present information positively
3. Consistency — Align messaging with actions
4. Likability — Humanize the brand
5. Unity — Create shared identity
